Free AI Text to Speech Online

AI Voice Studio Pro is a free online text-to-speech tool that converts written text into natural-sounding AI speech. Create voiceovers for YouTube videos, stories, documentaries, presentations, podcasts and other creative projects directly in your browser.

How to Convert Text to Speech

  • Paste or type your script into the text box.
  • Select a language, voice and narration style.
  • Adjust voice speed and pitch if needed.
  • Click Generate Voice, preview the audio and download the generated MP3.

AI Voice Generator for Stories and Videos

Choose narration styles including natural voice, story narration, horror, emotional, kids story, documentary, energetic and deep cinematic. The tool is designed for long scripts as well as shorter voiceovers.

Multiple Languages and Voices

AI Voice Studio Pro can provide voices for supported languages such as US English, UK English, Australian English, Indian English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada and Malayalam, depending on the available voice library.

Common Uses

Use text-to-speech audio for YouTube narration, animated stories, educational videos, documentaries, social media content, presentations, accessibility and creative audio projects.
