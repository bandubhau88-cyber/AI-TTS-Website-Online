AI Text to Speech Online

AI Voice Studio Pro converts written text into natural-sounding AI speech for YouTube videos, stories, documentaries, presentations and creative projects.

How it works

  • Create an account and receive 1,000 free characters.
  • Paste your text and select a language, voice and narration style.

Supported voices may include US English, UK English, Australian English, Indian English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada and Malayalam, depending on the available voice library.
